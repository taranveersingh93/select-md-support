# Privacy Policy: Select.md

Last updated: 2026-11-16

Select.md does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data or browsing data.

- **No servers.** The extension has no backend and makes no network requests.
- **Local processing only.** When you click Extract, the extension reads the current tab's page content in your browser, converts it to Markdown there, and shows it in the popup. Nothing leaves your device.
- **No tracking or analytics.** No cookies, identifiers, logs or analytics of any kind.
- **No stored data.** Your selections live only in the open page until you press Done, reload the page or close the tab. The extension does not save anything between sessions.
- **Downloads.** The `.md` file is saved only when you press Download, to the location you choose in the Save As dialog.

## Permissions and why they are needed
- `activeTab`: read the page you are viewing, only after you click the extension icon.
- `scripting`: run the extraction and selection code on that page.
- `downloads`: save the Markdown file you create.
