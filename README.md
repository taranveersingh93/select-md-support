# Select.md: support

Support and privacy information for **Select.md: Webpage to Markdown**, a Chrome extension that turns a webpage into a Markdown file. You choose exactly what goes in: click a section on the page, then move up or down the page structure to add what was missed or reject what you do not want.

## Get help
Found a bug or have a question or idea? [Open an issue](https://github.com/taranveersingh93/select-md-support/issues). If a page does not work well, include its URL and what you expected.

## Privacy
Select.md does not collect, store or transmit any personal or browsing data. Everything runs in your browser. Read the full [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
